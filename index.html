Standard Operating Protocol
The Never-Break Rules
Guaranteed viral formula — non-negotiable for all videos
1

Conflict Hook in First 2 Seconds

No warm-ups or greetings. The bottom 20 videos all lacked conflict hooks. The top 15 all opened with an immediate clash.

2

Location Overlay at 0:02 + Pinned Comment

Display shop location within the first 2 seconds and pin Google Maps link in comments. Captures local high-intent customers immediately.

3

Price Reveal Every Video

State exact price transparency: ("₱800 ra — dili ₱20,000"). High-contrast price relief converts viewers into actual repair leads.

4

ONE Call to Action (CTA) Per Video

Never confuse viewers with multiple demands. Part 1 = "Follow para di ma-miss ang Part 2". All other posts = "Send Messenger DM".

5

"Laing Shop" Stays Completely Generic

Never name or identify competitors. Maintain highest integrity and avoid legal or petty local drama.

6

Zero Customer Names or PII

Never expose customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or private diagnostic screens in camera frames.

7

DMs Are Always Human-Answered

Never automate DMs with robotic bots. The owner or trained staff replies personally. This is direct revenue.

8

Bislish Stays Yours

AI generates technical drafts, but native Cebuano speaker personally red-pens and approves every word before filming.
